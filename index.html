Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Our standard packages include accommodation, breakfast, sightseeing cab, and pickup/drop as mentioned. Entry fees to tourist spots, permits, and other personal expenses are typically not included unless explicitly stated in your custom quote.

A minimal token advance is required to block your rooms and cabs, especially during peak seasons. The remaining balance can be paid directly upon your arrival in Kodaikanal.

We offer free cancellation up to 7 days before your travel date. For cancellations within 3-7 days, a 50% refund is provided. There is no refund for cancellations made within 3 days of the travel date.

Yes! We specialize in organizing corporate trips, college tours, and large family get-togethers. We can arrange larger vehicles like tempo travelers or mini-buses and group accommodations at discounted rates.

Berijam Lake requires special permission from the Forest Department as it is a protected area. While it's not in the standard itinerary due to entry limits, we can arrange the permit and visit for an additional cost if informed in advance.

The best time to visit is from October to June. The weather is pleasantly cool and misty, perfect for sightseeing. September to November offers post-monsoon lush greenery, while summer (April-June) provides an escape from the heat.
